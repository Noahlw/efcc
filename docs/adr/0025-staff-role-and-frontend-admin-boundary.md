# Staff Role Vocabulary and Frontend Specification Boundary

**Status:** accepted (domain decision; implementation migration pending)

EFCC's canonical global roles are `Admin`, `Staff`, and `Member`; `Teacher` is retired as a product/domain term. This aligns the D1 model with the production `System_Role` vocabulary and makes `Staff` the replacement for the older D1/API spelling. The migration must preserve existing accounts while converting the stored/API role value and enforcing the canonical enum; self-service registration and approval remain `Member` by default, and elevated role assignment is a separate administrative capability. Permission Policy editing follows the hierarchy `Admin > Staff > Member`: Admin may edit Admin, Staff, and Member policies; Staff may edit Member policy; Member may edit none. Admin-policy edits must preserve at least one Admin policy editor, and a lower Role cannot edit its own or a higher Role's policy. The policy does not itself change an account's global Role or bypass server authorization.

`Program Leader` remains a per-Program relationship under the Program detail/management domain, never a global account Role. Issue #184 is the decision authority for the expanded Programs/Enrollment domain slice, including Program Detail relationships, Program Leader lifecycle, enrollment approval, and D1/Sheets ownership. The companion Program and Admin Management specification records the management surface and global role/policy contract, and consumes #184's domain decisions rather than redefining them. The current UI-rebuild specification covers role display, server-authorized Section visibility, hidden navigation for unavailable Sections, and direct-link Forbidden behavior.
