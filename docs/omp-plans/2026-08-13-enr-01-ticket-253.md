## Acceptance criteria

### ENR-01.1 — Queue states and counts are truthful

- The reachable Participants task exposes distinct Pending, Active, and historical/withdrawn views (or equivalent accessible filters/tabs).
- Counts are derived from the current scoped records and match the visible rows; pending is not conflated with active or historical withdrawal.
- Loading, empty, forbidden, stale, conflict, and server-error states are visible in the DOM and do not display fabricated success or stale counts after refresh.

### ENR-01.2 — Request decisions are atomic

- Approve a pending request inline. The response and refreshed DOM show the request decision and the corresponding active enrollment together, or neither when the operation fails.
- Reject a pending request inline. It leaves no active enrollment and appears in the historical decision view with the recorded reason/outcome.
- Attempt a second decision, decide a withdrawn/stale request, or use a request from another scope. The operation returns a deterministic conflict/forbidden/stale result and preserves the queue, active enrollment, and counts.
- Concurrent approvals of one request yield at most one active enrollment and one terminal request decision; the loser gets an explicit conflict/idempotent result.

### ENR-01.3 — Assisted enrollment enforces eligibility and scope

- A ManagerOnly operator can enroll an eligible Active Account in the current scoped program from the reachable Participants task.
- An inactive/nonexistent account, duplicate active enrollment, out-of-scope program/account, or non-ManagerOnly user is rejected before mutation with an explicit error.
- Successful assisted enrollment creates the active enrollment and its audit record; failed/conflicting attempts do not create a partial row and preserve current counts.

### ENR-01.4 — Audit and authorization are server-owned

- Worker/D1 responses and database state are the source of truth for decisions, assisted enrollment, counts, and audit outcomes; client-only state cannot claim success.
- Capability checks are applied on every mutation and scope is revalidated at mutation time, not only when the queue was loaded.
- No Apps Script or Google Sheet is mutated.

### ENR-01.5 — Reachability and accessibility

- A participant can submit a request in the browser and a manager can find and act on it without navigating through the legacy manager.
- All row actions have accessible names, keyboard operation, visible pending/error/success states, and work at narrow viewport widths.
